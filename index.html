Our Story

Grown with care.
Delivered with intention.

I started with one restaurant and grew from there — slowly, honestly, one kitchen at a time. Today Ute City Microgreens supplies some of the finest tables in the Roaring Fork Valley, and for the first time, is bringing that same quality straight to your door.

Everything is grown pesticide-free from non-GMO organic seed, cut fresh for every delivery, and dropped at your door free of charge. No middleman, no markup, no produce that's been sitting in a truck for days.

This is food grown with intention — for people who care what goes on their plate.
