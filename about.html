How It Began

Born from a passion
for exceptional food.

I started with one restaurant. That was it — one kitchen, one relationship, and a simple idea that what I was growing was worth sharing.

It grew from there, slowly and honestly. One restaurant became several. The grow room got bigger. Nothing fancy about it — just good product, delivered fresh every time.

Anyone who lives here knows that summer brings incredible farmers markets — but once the season ends, truly fresh local produce is just hard to find. I wanted to change that, at least for the people on my route.

I tried the store route for a while. It didn't sit right. By the time the product reached someone's hands, the store had doubled the price and the greens had lost half their life. That's not why I grow.

So this time I'm doing it differently — going straight to people's doors. Free delivery, once or twice a week, a curated mix of whatever is at its best. No middleman, no markup, just fresh greens the morning they're cut.

The Grow Room

Precision growing.
Every single tray.

Our grow room is a controlled environment built entirely around one goal: producing the most flavorful, most nutritious microgreens possible. Temperature, humidity, airflow, and light cycles are dialed in for each variety.

We use non-GMO organic seeds and grow on natural coconut coir medium with no synthetic additives. Every tray is monitored daily and harvested at the exact peak of nutritional density and flavor development.

This isn't factory farming — it's craft growing. Small batches, personal attention, and a standard that we hold to the same level as any fine dining kitchen.
